    Our story

    Three decades of sound, since 1995.

    AD Sound And System started in 1995 with a simple promise: every word clear, every beat felt. Since then we have successfully completed more than 1,500 events — weddings, garba nights, concerts, religious gatherings and corporate shows.

    Led by Dilipbhai Dave and Aniket Dave, our team plans, rigs and mixes every show in-house with premium JBL, British Acoustics, FANE, Midas, Yamaha and Soundcraft equipment.
